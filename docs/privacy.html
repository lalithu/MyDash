PRIVACY POLICY · OCTOBER 15, 2026

Your week belongs to you.

MyDash is an independent student-built Chrome extension maintained by Lalith Uriti. This policy describes the extension's handling of information.

Canvas and local planning

MyDash reads coursework information from your signed-in Canvas session to display assignments, course names, deadlines, and completion information. Planner settings, tasks, imported class schedules, and account-session information are stored using Chrome extension storage. Some settings use Chrome sync storage and may sync through your Google account according to your Chrome settings.

Optional MyDash account

Friends features use Supabase for authentication and data storage. When you create an account, your email, password-based authentication information, username, display name, and optional school are sent to Supabase. MyDash stores session tokens locally so you can remain signed in. Your Canvas password is not requested by MyDash.

Friends and schedule sharing

MyDash processes friend requests, accepted friendships, and the weekly schedule blocks you choose to share. Full sharing includes class titles, meeting times, and locations. Busy/free sharing sends time blocks without class titles or locations. Hidden sharing sends no schedule blocks. Shared non-class events use their category rather than their private title. You can change sharing in the schedule editor and remove friends in their detail view. Profile details are used to find people by username and identify friends and requests. Share only information you want available through these features.

Screenshot import

When supported by your Chrome profile and device, screenshot import uses Chrome's on-device model. The extension does not upload the screenshot to Supabase. Review the extracted schedule before saving; extraction can be incomplete or incorrect. Manual entry and .ics import are available alternatives.

Service data and third parties

Supabase processes account and social-feature requests and may retain operational logs such as IP addresses, request metadata, and timestamps. Chrome and Canvas operate under their own policies. MyDash does not include an advertising or analytics SDK in this release, and does not sell your data or use it for targeted advertising. Data is used to provide and maintain the planner and social features.

Your controls and deletion

You can edit your profile, remove friends, change sharing, and sign out on this device. Uninstalling removes extension-local data but does not delete your server-side MyDash account. To request account deletion and removal of associated profile, friendship, and shared-schedule data, email lalithuriti@gmail.com from your account email. Identity may need to be verified. Operational logs and provider backups follow the provider's retention practices. Do not include your password in a support request.

Security and retention

Network requests to Supabase use HTTPS. Account and social data remain while your account is active unless you edit, remove, or request deletion of them. No system can promise absolute security. Report security concerns privately to lalithuriti@gmail.com.

Contact and changes

Contact Lalith Uriti at lalithuriti@gmail.com for privacy questions. Material changes will be reflected in an updated policy date. MyDash is not affiliated with Instructure or UNC Charlotte.
